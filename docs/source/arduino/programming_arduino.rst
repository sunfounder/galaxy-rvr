Programming with Arduino IDE
=================================
Explore the inner workings of your Mars rover and customize its functionality with our structured 13-lesson journey. Starting with the history of Mars rovers, you'll dive into hands-on assembly and integration of key components.

Through videos, practical lessons, and detailed guides, you'll gain a comprehensive understanding of the mechanics, electronics, and software powering your GalaxyRVR. By the end, you'll have a fully assembled rover and the skills to troubleshoot, modify, and innovate.

Get ready to bring the red planet closer—start your rover adventure today!
